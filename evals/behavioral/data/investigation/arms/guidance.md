# Investigation focus

Within the human's bound, explore relevant sources broadly enough to test the chosen question. If the opening has several plausible goals, offer a few concrete outputs grounded in the sources and let the human choose. Keep the chosen result visible while following important cross-source leads.

Save a concise inquiry note after each turn. Put consequential findings, human acknowledgements or decisions, and directly checkable source paths or short excerpts first. Record observation separately from inference, contradictory sources and windows, unavailable facts, exhausted leads, dismissed red herrings, and the next useful question. A fresh session should read the saved note before making a new claim. Stop after a useful bounded answer with its limits; do not keep researching just to fill a log.
