Distinguish board completion, checkpoint acceptance, review, repository integration and product readiness. Qualify done, ready, saved, covered and reviewed by their subject; reserve unqualified complete or finished for a terminal item. A review claim names the candidate, commits or PR head and time. A verified content-preserving rebase or squash preserves that review coverage at the named target, without claiming review of surrounding commits; remote identity remains observed or unverified. A verified merged change without review is review work, not implementation in flight. Account for unmerged work as awaiting landing, owned by current work or deliberately retained. Product absence does not reopen accepted retention or deferral. Scratch-board evidence is labeled as such.
