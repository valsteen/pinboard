This route requires the human to request cancellation of a live item explicitly. A general cleanup or completion request, agent initiative, ambiguous wording and silence supply no authority. Resolve an ambiguous item with one concrete question before previewing. Every Pinboard client sharing the board must use a cancellation-aware build before its first cancellation; if that condition is unknown or cannot be met, stop cancellation and report it. Older builds cannot consume cancellation history, including validation and export. This condition authorizes no installation.

1. Call `pinboard_cancel` with one strict `request`: `operation: "preview"`, the exact `project_root`, `work_root` and `item_id`. Preview is read-only and acquires no authority. Preserve its returned `binding` unchanged.
2. Show the human the exact item ID, title, current state, objective and scope, accepted attempt and branch when present, preparation and worker ownership, and named prerequisites and dependents. Include the returned replacement coverage and bring uncovered obligations to the human; replacement coverage grants neither ending permission nor a veto against an explicit otherwise-legal ending. Explain all returned consequences: the item becomes dropped, retained evidence and repository files stay intact, prior Pinboard authority is fenced, and unsatisfied dependent obligations remain unsatisfied. Existing matching checkpoint acceptance evidence remains valid. Cancellation stops no arbitrary local process. Stop or await your own known worker before committing; a worker's future source-file writes are outside the ledger fence.
3. Ask for a **separate explicit confirmation of that exact item after the preview**, recommending cancellation only when it matches the human's stated request. End the turn and wait. An originating request, inferred intent, a general yes to another action, silence or expiry cannot serve as this confirmation. Retain the human's own exact confirmation words; never compose them yourself.
4. After confirmation, call `pinboard_cancel` with `operation: "confirm"`, the same exact roots and `item_id`, the returned `binding`, the human's words as `human_confirmation`, and your trusted current `actor_task_id` and `actor_host_id`. Do not use generic transition, ordinary close or completion as a cancellation substitute.
5. A different resolved board or changed item, definition, attempt, authority, displayed dependency fact or replacement coverage rejects unchanged. Read a fresh preview, show its current consequences and obtain a new separate confirmation; do not silently refresh the binding under old human words. After a committed result, preserve its receipt and read exact item status. A view warning requires its named repair, never replay. Report the commitment as cancelled/dropped, never completed or absorbed, and say whether retained code still needs repository disposition.

The canonical cancellation receipt records the exact displayed preview, binding, human confirmation and actor attribution. These are trusted-local records, not authenticated human authorship or proof of separate chat turns. The outer chat workflow owns the separate human exchange. Cancellation is available even with preparation, an accepted attempt, stale accepted scope or unresolved dependencies; cancellation creates no new prerequisite satisfaction and does not relax reviewed completion.
