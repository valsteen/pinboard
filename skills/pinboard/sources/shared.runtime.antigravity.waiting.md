After `invoke_subagent`, end the turn without polling or timers. Antigravity wakes the parent with the child's completion message. A prepared dispatch prompt is not a launched worker. Likewise, in any Antigravity session (coordinator, worker or reviewer), when a `run_command` call does not finish within `WaitMsBeforeAsync` and backgrounds as a task (`task-*`), end the turn immediately (call no further tools in that turn) and continue only after the system delivers the task completion notification; do not poll `manage_task` or issue overlapping `read_file` or `run_command` calls while the backgrounded command is running. Before ending its final turn, a worker or reviewer subagent must call `send_message` with the parent's conversation ID as `Recipient`.
